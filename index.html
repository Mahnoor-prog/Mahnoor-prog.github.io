Robust Road Object Detection in Fog and Low Light

◐ Research project
DataPublic road-scene dataset, 900 images, 5 classes
Stress testSynthetic fog and darkening (OpenCV, NumPy)
TrainYOLOv8n baseline vs fog/dark-augmented
ResultHeavy-fog car+person mAP50 0.81 → 0.98 (synthetic)

An experiment on how detectors degrade in low visibility. The baseline drops under heavy synthetic fog; adding fog and dark copies to the training set recovers it with no change on clean images. Limitations: the corruptions are synthetic, the test set has 100 images, and only car and person classes are scored. Next step: real foggy data, a dehazing comparison and architecture-level robustness. Full details are in the README.
